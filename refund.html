Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective November 2026 · Twenty Nine Cyber LLC, Orlando, Florida, USA

Free trial

The 21-day trial of 29Cyber CrewTrack and FieldProof is free and does not require a card. Nothing is charged when it ends.

App subscriptions

  • Plans are billed monthly in advance. You can cancel at any time by emailing fabio@29cyber.com; your plan stays active until the end of the month you already paid for.
  • We do not refund or credit partial months, unused days or unused seats.
  • Billing errors: if we charged you by mistake (for example, twice), we refund the wrong charge in full.
  • Our outages: if the Apps are unavailable for more than 3 consecutive days because of a problem on our side, ask us and we will credit the affected days on your next invoice.

Consulting services

  • Terms in your signed proposal take priority over this section.
  • If we ask for a deposit, it is refunded in full if you cancel at least 48 hours before the scheduled start.
  • After work begins, work already done is billed and any unused part of the deposit is refunded.
  • Hardware or licenses bought for you are not refundable by us once ordered; we will help you use the vendor's return policy when one exists.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days. If something is wrong with a charge, please contact us first; we answer within one business day.
